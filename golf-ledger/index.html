Special

12 courses

Trophy courses. Top 100 ranks, ultra-private clubs, and destination golf you'd brag about playing.

1
Cypress Point Club ★
Pebble Beach, CA · Alister MacKenzie & Robert Hunter 1928
#3 in America on Golf Digest's 100 Greatest. MacKenzie's finest work alongside Augusta National. ~250 members, invite-only, no commercial tournaments since 1990. Played June 11 — the 16th is the most iconic golf hole on Earth. Bogey on #16 and birdie on #8.
Top 3 AmericaUltra-Private
2
The Institute
Morgan Hill, CA · Bob Baldock / Fry family 2001
Arguably the most exclusive golf club in America — fewer than 50 members total, built by the Fry family on 1,500 acres in the Morgan Hill hills. Augusta-inspired holes, mandatory caddies, no Golf Digest ranking because panelists can't get on to score it. Played October 29.
<50 Members
